Editor basics

  • Posts are written in Markdown. The buttons above the text write it for you, and Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I and Ctrl+K work too.
  • Add pictures with the image button, or drop or paste them into the text. They are kept with the post, with their location data removed.
  • The cover shows at the top of the post and on the blog.
  • Pick the builds the post is about; readers see the ones you have shared.
