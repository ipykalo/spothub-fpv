SpotHub FPV is a hangar and logbook for FPV pilots, run by one person, {{ legal.operator }}. This page says what the site keeps about you, why, where, for how long, and how to have it deleted. Questions go to {{ legal.contactEmail }}.

What is kept, and why

From Google, when you sign in

  • Your Google account id — what your account is tied to, so you are recognised the next time.
  • Your email address — to check you are invited, while sign-up is by invitation. Only you and the site's admin see it.
  • Your name — shown to you and the admin. Everyone else sees the pilot name you choose on your account page, or your first name and the initial of your last.
  • The address of your profile picture — stored, but never shown or loaded by the site.

The site asks Google for nothing else, and never sees your Google password.

Where you signed in from

The country of the address you sign in from, looked up on the site's own server from a local database — your address is not sent anywhere and not stored. It is used to refuse sign-ins from a short list of restricted countries, and the latest one is kept with your account.

What you put in

Your builds, parts, prices and where you bought them, repairs, firmware snapshots, flight logs and the flights read from them — including GPS tracks, when a log has them — battery records, flying spots and their coordinates, posts, comments, likes and YouTube links, and your settings such as your pilot name and home currency. It is kept to show it back to you and to whoever you share it with.

Photos lose their location and camera details (EXIF) on the server as they are uploaded, so a photo taken at home does not carry your home's position.

Who sees it

  • Builds and spots are private until you share them: by link with pilots who are signed in, or listed for them. Cost and notes on a shared build stay hidden unless you switch them on.
  • A post is a draft until you publish it; a published post can be read by anyone.
  • A comment can be read by everyone who can open what it is on.
  • The admin sees a list of pilots: name, email, country of the last sign-in, when they joined and how much storage they use. The admin can technically reach everything stored, and looks only to run the site or when a problem is reported.

Cookies and your browser

One cookie, set when you sign in, keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is only ever sent to the site's sign-in address and cannot be read by scripts. Your browser also remembers your light or dark theme, which page sections you folded, and where to return after signing in — those never leave your device.

There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Google — only while you sign in.
  • OpenStreetMap — maps of spots and flights load their tiles from it, so it sees your address and which area you looked at.
  • YouTube — only once you press play on a video, and through its privacy-enhanced player.
  • Google Maps — only if you tap Directions on a spot.

Where it is kept

The site and its backups are hosted in the EU. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

How long, and deleting it

Everything is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account yourself on your account page: everything you put in — builds, parts, flights, logs, photos, spots, posts, comments and likes — is deleted at once.

Copies in the backups go after that: your files within a day, and the database backups as they age out, at the latest 8 weeks later. A backup is only ever read to restore the site after a failure.

Your rights

You can see, correct and delete what the site keeps about you — most of it on the site itself. To get a copy of your data, have something corrected that you cannot change yourself, or to object to how it is used, write to {{ legal.contactEmail }}. If you are in the EU you can also complain to your country's data protection authority.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that matters is announced on the blog first. The terms of use cover what you may post.
